FIELD NOTE · SITE STRUCTURE

A practical guide to clear site structure.

6 minute read · Crawl Lab editorial team

Start with a visitor's question

Group pages around questions people ask. Give important topics a clear home, then link supporting pages from that hub. A well-organized foundation makes the next step obvious.

Use links that explain themselves

Descriptive link text helps people know where a click leads. Connect related articles and keep important content reachable from another page.

Review paths over time

Check for pages without inbound links and for repeated information. Continue with metadata basics.
